# Elo-Duell – quick guide

Elo-Duell helps you find out what you really like best from a list of options (holiday destinations, restaurants, films …). You always compare just two options; a ranking builds up from that. As a pair, you swap rankings and instantly see where you agree.

**App:** https://user-2268431.github.io/elo-duell/

**Language:** German/English, chosen automatically from your phone's language, switchable in Settings (“Language / Sprache”).

## Install on Android in 3 steps

1. Open the address above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the menu **⋮** (top right) and choose **“Install app”** (on some devices **“Add to home screen”**).
3. Confirm with **“Install”** or **“Add”**. Elo-Duell is now on your home screen and also works offline after the first start.

## Use

- **New list:** enter a name and paste options, one per line.
- **Duel:** tap the option you prefer. “Equally good” counts as a draw, “Skip” shows another pair, “Undo” takes back the last duel.
- **Progress:** first the ranking is *provisional*, after enough duels it is *stable*. The bar and hint tell you roughly how many duels are left.
- **Ranking:** all options start at 1,000 points; winners take points from losers.
- **Images:** photos from Wikipedia (in the app language first). The terms are sent to Wikipedia; you can turn this off in Settings.

## As a pair

1. Share the list with each other first (“Share” → QR code or link).
2. Both of you duel on your own phone.
3. Both share your ranking: the other person scans the QR code (“Scan QR”) or opens the link.
4. The comparison shows **Shared favourites**, **Clear no-gos** and topics **Worth discussing**.

A German and an English phone can exchange lists and rankings – the data does not depend on the language.

## Backup

Your data stays only on your phone. In **Settings → Backup** you can export everything as a file (e.g. before switching phones) and import it again. Importing replaces all lists on the device.
